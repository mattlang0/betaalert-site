BetaAlert

Hear the hazard before you see it.

BetaAlert is an iPhone app for whitewater paddlers and mountain bikers. Mark the holes, strainers, drops and blind corners of a run ahead of time, start the run, and get a voice or tone alert as you approach each one, with the phone locked in a dry bag or a pocket.

Share a run with your group by QR code or file. Everyone gets their own editable copy. Works without a signal.
