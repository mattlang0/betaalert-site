BetaAlert

Privacy policy

BetaAlert collects no data. Everything you put into the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your runs, the points you mark, their names and notes, and any emergency info you type are stored in the app's database on your iPhone. They are never sent to us. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your iPhone, the backup includes it, under your own Apple ID.

Location

BetaAlert reads your location on the phone to tell how far you are from each point and to sound an alert as you approach. While a run is active it keeps reading location in the background, so alerts work with the phone locked. Your location is used only on the phone and is stored only as part of the runs you create.

Dictation

If you dictate a point's description, BetaAlert uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition. On iPhones that support it, recognition happens entirely on the phone. On others, Apple's speech service processes the audio under Apple's privacy policy. Dictation only runs while you hold the button.

Apple Maps

Three features send a location or search text to Apple Maps and get places back: searching for a place on the map, naming a new run after where it is, and looking up nearby hospitals, emergency services and access points for a run's emergency info. That lookup may also run in the background when the phone has a signal. These requests go to Apple, not to us, and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

You can share a run as a QR code or a file. The shared run contains the points, names, notes and emergency info you chose to include. It goes only where you send it, through whatever app you share it with. Someone who opens it gets their own copy on their phone.

Children

BetaAlert is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
